Research Profile

I am Zile Zhang, a Ph.D. at Wenzhou University, focusing on advanced metallic materials, particularly high-entropy alloys and magnetic materials. My work centers on understanding composition–structure–property relationships and exploring practical strategies for alloy design and performance optimization. Beyond materials research, I maintain a long-term interest in competitive gaming and in the surprisingly effective links between scientific workflow, tactical judgment, iteration, timing, and execution.
